# CVD-of-Bi-O-Se-Thin-Films-on-Silicon-Substrates
Investigated the CVD growth of Bi₂O₂Se thin films on silicon substrates, focusing on controlled deposition conditions and thin-film formation.
Investigated the chemical vapour deposition (CVD) growth of Bi₂O₂Se thin films on Si substrates, focusing on controlled thin-film deposition and material formation.
Studied the influence of **growth parameters such as precursor conditions, deposition temperature, carrier-gas flow, and reaction environment** on film quality and morphology.
Performed **structural, morphological, and compositional characterization** using techniques including **XRD, SEM, EDS, and FTIR** to evaluate crystallinity, surface morphology, elemental composition, and chemical bonding.
Correlated **CVD process parameters with thin-film properties** to understand their effect on uniformity, crystallinity, and surface characteristics.
Developed practical understanding of **thin-film synthesis, vacuum/gas-flow-based processing, substrate preparation, and materials characterization** relevant to semiconductor and advanced-material applications.
